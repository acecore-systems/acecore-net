When choosing a person image for a website, think about what the scene needs to communicate. Acecore Cast shows the images used across our sites. Acecore Pick shows candidate images one at a time with an intended use.

## Compare images in Cast

Open a cast member in [Acecore Cast](https://cast.acecore.net/) to see published images with different angles and expressions. The people shown are AI-generated illustrations. See the catalogue’s terms and contact details if you need to check permitted uses.

## Review a candidate in Pick

Read the intended use on the card in [Acecore Pick](https://pick.acecore.net/), then choose left or right to say whether the image fits. You can skip an image. Press and hold a rating button to add a reason. A comment such as “the expression looks natural, but it is hard to see the work” explains what matters for the scene.
