The [company profile](/en/about/) lists the legal name as Kabushiki Kaisha Acecore and introduces the executives and their areas of responsibility. It offers a starting point for understanding who works on each part of Acecore.

Acecore Systems focuses on technology support, Acecore Schools on learning support, Aceserver on its Minecraft community, and Acecore Store on official merchandise. Follow the links from the company profile to each specialist site for current offerings and terms.

See the [business guide](/en/services/) for an overview. For inquiries that span several areas or proposals to collaborate, [contact Acecore](/en/contact/).
