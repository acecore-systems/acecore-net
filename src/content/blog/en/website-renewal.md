We have renewed the Acecore website as a corporate site for company information and access to our businesses. Here is what we reorganized and the kinds of announcements we will publish.

## What changed

We clarified where company-wide information and detailed service information belong. Company information and business entry points are collected here, while prices, service scope, and participation conditions are available on specialist sites.

This reduces repeated explanations and helps readers reach information suited to their purpose. See the [business page](/en/services/) for current departments and services.

## What company news will cover

We publish company information updates, activities across businesses, and important announcements. Technical articles, educational content, and detailed community activity records remain on the relevant specialist sites.

Website changes like this renewal will also be announced in company news.
