We have revamped the official Acecore website into a corporate site that makes it easy to find company information and navigate to each of our businesses. Below, we explain what we reorganized and what announcements we will publish on this site.

## What we reorganized in the redesign

We clarified where company-wide information and detailed service information belong. Company information and links to our businesses are collected here, while details such as pricing, service scope, and participation requirements are available on specialist sites.

This reduces repeated explanations across multiple locations and helps readers find information that suits their needs. See the [business page](/en/services/) for current departments and services.

## What company news will cover

We will share company information updates, activities spanning multiple businesses, and important announcements. Technical explainers, educational content, and detailed records of community activities will be published on the respective specialist sites.

We will also announce website changes like this one in company news.
