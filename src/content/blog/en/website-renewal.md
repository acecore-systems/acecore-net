We have revamped the Acecore official website as a corporate site where you can find company information and gateways to each of our businesses. This guide explains how to find information and how this site and our specialist sites divide their roles, for those who want to learn about the company, find a specific service, or read updates on our activities.

## Find information by purpose

Use [Company Overview](/about/) for the company profile, [Business Overview](/services/) for an overview of all our businesses, and [News](/blog/) for company-wide updates. If you are looking for specific prices or participation requirements, continue to the relevant specialist site. By narrowing your search to the field you are interested in, you can more easily compare information about the company as a whole with specific offerings.

## We have clarified the roles of our official sites

acecore.net carries the company profile, a list of Acecore's businesses, and corporate announcements. Detailed specialist information is consolidated on the official website managed by each business.

- [Acecore Systems](https://systems.acecore.net/): development, production, and operations support, pricing, case studies, and technical articles
- [Acecore Schools](https://schools.acecore.net/): learning support, pricing, how to seek advice, and activity reports
- [Aceserver](https://asv.acecore.net/): how to join the Minecraft community, rules, and activity logs

This division means pricing and descriptions do not need to be maintained across multiple sites. We direct visitors to each official site as the authoritative source for the latest information.

## Corporate news

The news section on acecore.net covers company information, cross-business activities, and important announcements. Technical articles, educational content, and detailed community updates will continue to be published on each specialist site.

For inquiries spanning multiple businesses or partnership proposals, please contact us via [Contact](/contact/).

It can also serve as a starting point when reviewing your own website and planning a structure that helps readers find the information they need.
