Whether you need a character design, a 3D model, writing or audio, the Acecore Designs site helps you find the right production service and where to ask about it.

## Start with a work example

In the [portfolio](https://designs.acecore.net/en/works/), you can see work such as Harushiyu, a 3D model created by Hatt, and read about what was made. Use the [service list](https://designs.acecore.net/en/services/) to explore illustration, 3D, and writing or audio.

![Portfolio image of Harushiyu, a 3D model created by Hatt](/uploads/editorial-20261117/designs-harushiyu.jpg)

## What to prepare before asking

Note where the work will appear, what you need delivered, and your timing. When supplying a reference image, explain which parts match your idea. Check the [Acecore Designs site](https://designs.acecore.net/en/) for current scope and usage terms.
