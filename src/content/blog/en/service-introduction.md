Want to discuss a website or business IT, think about how to learn, or play Minecraft? The right place to start depends on your goal. This guide introduces Acecore's businesses by purpose and directs you to official sites where you can check detailed offerings and current terms.

## What to clarify when you're unsure where to turn

Systems is the place to start for IT and web inquiries, Schools for learning inquiries, Aceserver for joining the Minecraft community, and Store for questions about products or shipping. If your inquiry involves multiple businesses, jot down what you want to achieve, what you're currently having trouble with, and your preferred timeframe. For detailed specifications and estimates, please contact the relevant specialist team.

## Acecore Systems

Information about IT support, system and web development, design, operational support, pricing, case studies, and technical guides is available at [Acecore Systems](https://systems.acecore.net/).

## Acecore Schools

Information about learning approaches, educational support, ways to consult, and activity reports is available at [Acecore Schools](https://schools.acecore.net/).

## Aceserver

Information about how to join the Minecraft community, related services, and the latest activity updates is available at the [official Aceserver portal](https://asv.acecore.net/).

## Acecore Store

The latest information about products, how to purchase them, shipping, and returns is available at [Acecore Store](https://store.acecore.net/).

## Contact Acecore

For company information, see [Company Profile](/about/). For inquiries spanning multiple businesses or collaboration, see [Contact](/contact/).

## A resource to consult before discussing IT

If you want to organize issues with IT operations, the [IPA Guidelines for Information Security Measures in Small and Medium-sized Enterprises](https://www.ipa.go.jp/security/guide/sme/about.html) may also be helpful. They separate guidance for management from procedures for advancing measures within the organization, and can serve as a resource for considering what your company should tackle first.
