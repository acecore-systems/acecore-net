Before contacting Acecore, outline what you want to achieve, what is difficult today, and your preferred timing. This article explains how to prepare for the conversation.

## Three things to clarify

1. **Your goal**: Briefly describe what you want to change and who it is for.
2. **Current difficulties**: Describe your current process and specific situations that take effort or cause inconvenience.
3. **Preferred timing**: Note target dates for starting or launching, and where the schedule is flexible.

For example, if you want a website, include its intended audience, the information you want to publish, and your preferred launch date. Discuss detailed specifications and estimates with the specialist team based on this context.

## Find the right contact

The [business page](/en/services/) lists current departments, services, and where to check prices. Use it to reach the relevant specialist team. For matters spanning several areas or if you are unsure where to ask, use the [general contact form](/en/contact/).
