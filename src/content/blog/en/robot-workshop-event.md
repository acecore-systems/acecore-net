In summer 2023, Acecore Schools held an in-person robot-building workshop for elementary and middle school students. This limited summer event let participants experience making by assembling mechanisms, customizing them, and checking how they moved.

## What We Held in Summer 2023

- **Age range**: Elementary to middle school students (younger children by consultation)
- **Venue**: Sakuradai Community Center (3F, Lecture Room 2)
- **Dates**: August 20 and 27, 2023
- **Time**: 2:00 PM start each day (90 minutes)
- **Schedule**: 45 minutes of building + 45 minutes of customization and motion testing
- **Fee**: ¥2,000 per person per session (materials included)
- **Materials**: Tamiya Fun Craft Series (take-home)
- **Capacity**: 10 participants per session (reservation required)
- The finished project could also be used for a **summer independent study project**.

## Offer Available at the Time

At the time, participants who registered on LINE received a ¥200 discount.

## What the Workshop Was Like

Participants concentrated on assembling their robots, then became especially engaged while customizing and testing them. Hearing everyone cheer when a robot began to move was also a memorable moment for the staff.

By building, customizing, and testing one step at a time, participants could explore how the mechanisms worked with their own hands. This was not a programming class; it was held as a making experience focused on robot mechanisms and movement.
