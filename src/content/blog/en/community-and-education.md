Acecore operates dedicated sites for its business, learning, and community activities. This page outlines these activities and points you to official sources for the latest information.

## Aceserver

**Aceserver** is a Minecraft community operated by Acecore. How to join, the rules, and the latest activity updates are available on the [official Aceserver portal](https://asv.acecore.net/).

You can read Alpha-kun’s illustrated diary by choosing a date, starting with the [guide for first-time visitors](https://asv.acecore.net/stories/alpha-diary-guide/).

## Acecore Schools

**Acecore Schools** is an education business that designs learning around each learner’s goals and where they are now. Information about who it serves, learning formats, fees, and how to consult with the team is available on the [official Acecore Schools website](https://schools.acecore.net/).

A past hands-on robot-building workshop is featured as an [activity record](https://schools.acecore.net/activities/2023-summer-robot-workshop/).

## Open Source and Collaboration

Public projects from Acecore Systems are available on [GitHub](https://github.com/acecore-systems). Details of technical support and technical explainers are available on [Acecore Systems](https://systems.acecore.net/).

The published [Codex Task Routing](https://github.com/acecore-systems/codex-task-routing) project covers the design of task assignment and verification for Codex. The background is summarized in a [technical explainer on Systems](https://systems.acecore.net/insights/codex-task-routing-design/).

For cross-business collaboration or joint projects, please contact Acecore via its [contact page](/contact/).
