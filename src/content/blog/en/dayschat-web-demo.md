If you would like to try a short English reply or write about your day, open the [DaysChat Web demo](https://dayschat.acecore.net/) to explore chat and diary controls.

## Reply to a sample and check your diary

Try a reply to one of the supplied examples. Change the difficulty setting and open the vocabulary record. Write a diary entry and save it to reopen in the same browser. To focus on the controls, start with example text you do not mind sharing.

[DaysChat Web chat screen at mobile width](/uploads/editorial-20261117/dayschat-web.jpg)

## What the demo supports

The demo uses fixed examples and stores records in your browser. Live AI replies to free-form input, sign-in and syncing between devices are outside this demo.
